# 8.5 Third party licensing \{#8-5-third-party-licensing\}

MediatR (from version 13) and MassTransit (from v9) have moved to commercial licenses; confirm licensing before each project starts:

- **MediatR**: organisations with annual revenue under USD 5 million and no more than USD 10 million in outside funding can use the free Community license. Eligibility is judged by **the organisation that actually owns and uses the system**.
- **MassTransit v9**: organisations with annual revenue under USD 1 million **may** receive a full discount, but the official wording is "may", so apply and confirm. If the system is later handed to another organisation to maintain, that organisation must obtain its own license.
- **MassTransit v8**: still free under Apache 2.0, but official maintenance ends at the end of 2026; not recommended for new projects.
- **FluentAssertions**: from version 8, commercial use requires a paid license. The English guide uses FluentAssertions 8; this template does not include it and relies on xUnit's built in `Assert`. For more readable assertions, consider the open source Shouldly.
